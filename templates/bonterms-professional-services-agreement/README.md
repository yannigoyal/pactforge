# Bonterms Professional Services Agreement

A balanced Professional Services Agreement (PSA) drafted by the
[Bonterms Standard Agreement Committee](https://bonterms.com/committee/).

- **Template file:** [`Professional-Services-Agreement.md`](./Professional-Services-Agreement.md) (Version 1.2)
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](./LICENSE.md)
- **Source:** [Bonterms/Professional-Services-Agreement](https://github.com/Bonterms/Professional-Services-Agreement) (imported as-is, unmodified)

## Usage

The PSA is designed to be used together with a signed Cover Page that identifies the
parties (Customer and Provider) and Key Terms (Effective Date, Rights in Deliverables,
Governing Law and Courts). Services themselves are ordered via SOWs that reference the
Agreement. See the [Bonterms PSA repository](https://github.com/Bonterms/Professional-Services-Agreement)
for an example Cover Page and additional guidance.
